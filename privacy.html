Privacy notice

This is a working draft for JOJO & FLO. The salon should review it and amend anything that does not match how they actually use client data.

Controller: JOJO & FLO, 310 Green Lanes, Palmers Green, London N13 5TT. Email: hello@jojoandflo.com. Telephone: 020 8882 6400.

What we collect

Depending on how you use the salon and website, we may hold:

  • Name, phone number, and email when you book, enquire, or request the price list
  • Appointment history, notes about your hair, and patch-test or waiver records
  • Payment details processed by our card or shop providers (we do not store full card numbers on this website)
  • Online shop details handled by PaySynk at checkout
  • Messages you send us by email or social media
  • Technical data such as cookie preferences stored on your device

Why we use it

  • To book, confirm, and carry out salon services
  • To take payment, issue vouchers, and fulfil product orders
  • To keep health and safety records, including patch tests and declined-test waivers
  • To send information you asked for, such as the price list
  • To improve the website and remember essential preferences
  • To meet legal, accounting, and insurance requirements

We rely on a mix of contract (your booking or purchase), legitimate interests (running a salon safely), legal obligation, and consent where you have chosen optional cookies or marketing.

Who we share it with

We only share what is needed with trusted providers who help us run the business, for example:

  • Our booking system
  • PaySynk for the online shop and cart
  • Email delivery for forms on this website
  • Payment processors
  • Professional advisers, insurers, or authorities if required by law

We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

Salon records are kept for as long as we need them to look after you and to meet legal or insurance timescales. Website form submissions (price list requests and patch-test waivers) are kept so we have a record of what was sent. You can ask us to review or delete information where the law allows.

Cookies

This website uses a small number of cookies and similar storage:

  • Essential — remember your cookie choice, keep the shop cart working, and remember if you have unlocked the price list
  • Optional — we do not currently run advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this notice and ask again

You can accept or refuse optional cookies in the bar at the bottom of the site. Essential storage is needed for the site to work as expected.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, restrict or object to certain uses, and receive a copy of data you provided. You can also withdraw consent where we rely on it. These rights are not always absolute — we will explain if we cannot fully meet a request.

To make a request, email hello@jojoandflo.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

This website is not aimed at children. If we need information about a child for a salon appointment, we collect it from a parent or guardian.

Updates

We may update this notice when our services or the law change. The latest version will always be on this page.
