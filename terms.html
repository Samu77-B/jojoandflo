Salon policy

Terms & conditions

Please read these terms before booking or attending an appointment. JOJO & FLO may update this page from time to time.

Appointments

Appointments can be booked online, by phone, or in salon. Please arrive on time so we can give you the full service. If you are more than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten or reschedule your appointment so later guests are not delayed.

Cancellations & no-shows

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or change an appointment. Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged, at the salon’s discretion, because that time has been held for you.

Consultations

A consultation is £40 and is redeemable against your hair service. Colour and chemical work is always discussed first so we can agree a suitable result for your hair, lifestyle, and previous treatments.

Patch testing

For colour, bleaching, and other chemical services we recommend a skin allergy alert test (patch test) at least 48 hours before your appointment. This is to reduce the risk of an allergic reaction.

  • Please tell us about previous reactions, sensitive skin, or any change in your health or medication.
  • A patch test does not guarantee that a reaction will not occur on the day, but declining a test increases that risk.
  • We may refuse a chemical service if a patch test has not been carried out and a waiver has not been completed.

If you have been offered a patch test and you choose to go ahead without one, please complete the e-sign waiver below. A copy is emailed to the salon for our records.

Colour & chemical services

Hair that has been previously coloured, bleached, or treated at home can respond unpredictably. We will always advise you honestly if we cannot achieve a result safely in one visit. Additional appointments or product may be needed, and these are charged accordingly.

Children

Please speak to the salon before booking for children. We cannot look after unattended children in the salon.

Payments & retail

Payment is taken after your service, unless a deposit has been requested. Gift vouchers are available for collection in salon. Online product orders are handled by our shop provider; please see the checkout terms at the time of purchase.

Personal belongings

Please keep valuables with you. We cannot accept liability for loss or damage to personal items left in the salon.

Photographs

We may occasionally photograph finished work for our gallery or social channels. If you would prefer not to be photographed, tell your stylist — we will always respect that.
