Who we are

Our Story

JOJO & FLO was founded by sisters Joanna and Florie Yerou, two of the UK's leading hairdressers. With over 30 years of combined experience, they began their careers at the world-renowned John Frieda salon in London's Mayfair, progressing from apprentices to Senior Stylists before becoming educators at the John Frieda Training School.

Our Experience & Editorial Work

Their work has taken them backstage at London Fashion Week, styling hair for designers including Alexander McQueen, Calvin Klein, Valentino, Betty Jackson, Ben de Lisi, and Amanda Wakeley. Their work has featured in publications such as Vogue and ELLE, as well as across film and television productions including Eyes Wide Shut and Comic Relief.

Over the years, Joanna and Florie have styled a diverse range of high-profile clients, including Tom Cruise, Nicole Kidman, Yasmin Le Bon, David Furnish, Sir Tim Rice, Noel Gallagher, Jamie Oliver, French & Saunders, and members of international royal households.

The Vision

After a decade in Mayfair, the sisters set out to create something more personal: bringing high-end, editorial-standard hairdressing into a relaxed, welcoming community setting. That vision became JOJO & FLO, established in Palmers Green, North London (N13). Their goal was to give clients of all ages access to Mayfair-level expertise, precision cutting, and expert colour work without needing to travel into central London.

The Consultation & Craftsmanship

Every appointment starts with an in-depth consultation. The team takes the time to understand your features, hair type, lifestyle, and daily routine before creating a cut, colour, or complete transformation tailored specifically to you. The focus is on technical excellence, craftsmanship, and creating beautiful, wearable hair that fits effortlessly into everyday life and is easy to maintain long after leaving the salon.

The Salon & Products

Located in N13, the salon space has been designed as a calm, elegant, and modern retreat where clients can relax and enjoy a bespoke experience. JOJO & FLO is equally passionate about product quality, recommending carefully selected luxury products chosen for their performance, quality, and hair health benefits.

True luxury isn't about extravagance—it is about exceptional service, attention to detail, and creating beautiful, healthy hair that is uniquely yours.
